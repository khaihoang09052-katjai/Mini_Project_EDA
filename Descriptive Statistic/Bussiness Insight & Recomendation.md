# Customer Churn: Business Insights and Recommendations

The cleaned dataset contains **379,216 customers**, including **189,282 who churned (49.91%)**. All records belonging to repeated customer IDs were excluded from this analysis.

## Main Findings

- **Support calls:** Churn reaches **93.08% at five calls** and stays around **99.8% at six or more calls**. Follow up on repeated requests and check whether the underlying issue has been resolved.
- **Payment delays:** Churn stays around **40% up to 20 days overdue**, then rises above **99% in the 21-30 day groups**. Review payment failures and test reminders or assistance before delays become prolonged.
- **Contract length:** Monthly contracts have **99.79% churn**, compared with **39.38% for Annual** and **39.34% for Quarterly**. Check the monthly renewal experience and test voluntary renewal offers.
- **Engagement and spending:** Lower usage, a longer time since the last interaction and lower spending are associated with higher churn. For example, churn is **60.23%** for customers last active 22-30 days ago, compared with about **42.5%** for those active within 14 days.

## Suggested Next Step

Start with small tests of support follow-up, payment assistance and renewal offers. Compare retention, successful payments, renewals and costs against the current process before expanding. Use age and gender findings as context rather than as the sole basis for offers.

## Scope

These results describe the cleaned subset. Excluding repeated IDs may change which customers are represented, and the relationships do not prove what causes churn. Confirm the churn definition and when each variable was recorded, especially for groups with rates close to 100%.
